Privacy Policy

CookMate AI · Last updated 9 August 2026

This policy explains what CookMate AI (com.urooj.cookmate) collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers the Android application only.

Who is responsible

CookMate AI is an independently developed application. For any privacy question or request described in this policy, contact: uroojshehzadi03@gmail.com.

What the app collects

DataWhyWhen
Email address Identifies your account and allows sign-in and password reset. On registration or Google Sign-In.
Display name Shown in the app so it can greet you. You provide it; Google Sign-In supplies it automatically.
Profile photo (optional) Shown as your avatar. Only if you choose one from your gallery.
Saved and favourited recipes So your collection is there on your next sign-in. When you save or favourite something.
AI prompts and conversations Generates the recipe or reply you asked for, and keeps your chat history. When you use the AI features.
AI usage totals Powers the in-app Credit Usage screen so you can see your own consumption. After each AI request.
Notification token Allows the app to receive push notifications. If you grant notification permission.
Crash and error diagnostics Identifies faults so they can be fixed. Only when the app encounters an error.
The app does not collect your location, contacts, calendar, microphone, or camera. It contains no advertising and no advertising identifiers. Your data is not sold, rented, or shared for marketing.

Where the data goes

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics) stores your account and content. See the Firebase privacy documentation.
  • Google Gemini API receives the text of prompts you send to the AI features so it can generate a response. Do not include information in a prompt that you would not want processed by a third-party AI service. See Google's privacy policy.
  • TheMealDB and Wikimedia Commons supply recipe images. These are ordinary image requests and carry no account data.

What stays only on your device

  • Your theme, text size, and notification preferences.
  • Your notification inbox.
  • Cached recipe images, which exist to reduce data use and reload time.

Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all associated data from inside the app: Profile → Settings → Delete Account. It asks you to confirm and to re-authenticate, because the action cannot be undone.

Deletion removes your profile, saved and favourited recipes, AI conversations, usage records, and your authentication account. Backups may retain copies for a short period before being overwritten. If you cannot access the app, email the address above from your registered address and the same deletion will be performed.

Retention

Account data is kept while your account exists. Delete the account and it is removed as described above. Crash diagnostics are retained by Firebase Crashlytics under Google's own retention schedule.

Children

CookMate AI is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are not knowingly created for them. If you believe a child has registered, contact the address above and the account will be removed.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Database rules restrict every record to the signed-in account that owns it. No system is perfectly secure, so this is a description of the measures taken rather than a guarantee.

Your choices

  • Notifications can be turned off in Settings or in Android's own settings.
  • A profile photo is optional and can be removed.
  • Chat history can be deleted per conversation from the AI screen.
  • The AI usage log can be cleared from the Credit Usage screen.
  • The whole account can be deleted as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be signalled in the app.
