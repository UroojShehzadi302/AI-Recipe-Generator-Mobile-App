Delete Your Account

CookMate AI · Last updated 17 August 2026

This page explains how to permanently delete your CookMate AI (com.urooj.cookmate) account and the data attached to it. CookMate AI is an independently developed Android application by Urooj Shehzadi.

Option 1 — delete it yourself, inside the app

This is immediate and does not require anyone's approval.

  1. Open CookMate AI and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Open Settings, then tap Delete Account under the Account section. (You can also reach it from the Account menu on the Profile screen.)
  4. Type DELETE in the confirmation field.
  5. Confirm who you are — enter your password, or sign in with Google again if you registered with a Google account. This step is required by Firebase for security and cannot be skipped.
  6. Tap the delete button. Your data is erased and you are signed out.
The deletion runs immediately. It cannot be undone, and there is no grace period during which the account can be recovered.

Option 2 — ask us to delete it

If you can no longer install or sign in to the app, email uroojshehzadi03@gmail.com from the address you registered with, using the subject line Delete my CookMate AI account.

We reply within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm the request from the registered email address before acting on it, so that nobody else can delete your account.

What is kept, and for how long

DataRetention
Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) Kept up to 90 days by Google, then deleted automatically. These are diagnostic traces — they are not linked to your name or email address and cannot be used to identify you after your account is gone.
Prompts you sent to the AI assistant Your copy of every conversation is deleted with your account. However, prompts were processed by Google's Gemini API when you sent them, and that processing is governed by Google's own retention policy — we cannot delete data held on Google's servers on your behalf.
Notification records stored on your device Held only in the app's local storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data; they are never uploaded.
Deleting your account does not uninstall the app. To remove it completely, uninstall CookMate AI from your device after deleting your account.
